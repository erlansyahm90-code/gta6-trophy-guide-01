    Read this before you play: 1 trophy can be missed

    Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot across Leonida) is the only trophy GTABase and PowerPyx flag as possibly missable. The first meditation is Lucia’s on the balcony at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, the morning after Down to Earth — and it is reported to disappear once Chapter 1 ends. Do it the moment it is offered, and keep a manual save from the start of every chapter: GTA VI has no mission replay and no New Game+, but 15 manual save slots. Cheats disable trophies and saving for the session, so leave them off for a trophy run.
